# Decision Procedure for RNT as a Backend of the ADP Framework

The five examples in this directory show that a combination of the decision procedure for RNT PTRSs
and the ADP framework increases the overall power. 
They are not right-ground, so the decision procedure for AST of RNT PTRSs is not applicable directly.
In the ADP framework, the initial rules are separated from the core, and the underlying PTRS of the remaining sub-problem is in RNT. 
The cores are critical branching processes that cannot be handled by polynomial interpretations, but they are decided by the decision procedure.
